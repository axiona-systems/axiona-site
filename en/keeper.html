AXIONA KEEPERAXIONA PRODUCT
IN DEVELOPMENTiPhone + iPad · Apple App Store

Bring in the document. Keeper takes care of much of the admin around it.

Whether it is a PDF or a photo of a document, Keeper reads it, tries to identify what it is, gives you a short summary, pulls out important facts and dates, works out what it may belong to and flags anything that may need action. You should not have to read every document end to end, extract the important details, piece together the relationships and remember every deadline yourself.

If Keeper is not sure about something, it tells you. You can correct it, and reminders or other next steps only happen after you approve them.

WHY THIS MATTERS

The point is not just to store the document. It is to take the admin around it off your hands.

With basic storage, the file is kept but the admin is still yours: reading it, finding the important details, working out what it belongs to and remembering deadlines. Keeper is designed to do most of those steps for you and ask for your review only where it is actually needed.

WHAT THIS LOOKS LIKE IN PRACTICE

The same help, across very different documents.

A few everyday examples of the kind of admin Keeper can take off your hands.

INSURANCE LETTER

You should not have to hunt for the claim number and deadline yourself.

An insurance letter comes in. Keeper can help bring the details you need for the claim straight to the surface.

KEEPER WORK
  • recognize that it may be an insurance document
  • surface the reference and possible deadline
  • work out which claim or vehicle it may belong to
  • tell you when a reply or follow-up may be needed
INVOICE / PAYMENT NOTICE

You should not have to dig out the amount and due date yourself.

Keeper can bring the issuer, amount and payment deadline forward so you do not have to reread the invoice to find them.

KEEPER WORK
  • pull out the issuer, amount and date
  • recognize that it may be an invoice or payment notice
  • show where the deadline appears in the document
  • suggest a reminder for you to approve
CONTRACT / SUBSCRIPTION

A contract may later receive amendments, invoices or fresh notices.

KEEPER WORK

  • surface relevant dates

  • flag when review or expiry may be approaching
PROPERTY / RENOVATION

Quotes, invoices, photos and warranties may belong to the same work even when they arrive in different formats.

TRUST AND CONTROL

Keeper helps, but the final say stays with you.

01

Designed to work on your device where possible.

The default direction is to process documents locally instead of sending them elsewhere without a clear need.

02

See where an important detail came from.

For an important date or amount, you should be able to go back to the part of the document Keeper read it from.

03

If Keeper is not sure, it should say so.

If something cannot be recognized confidently enough, Keeper should ask you to check it.

04

If you correct it, that correction should stick.

A later automatic suggestion should not quietly undo a correction you already made.
