AXIONA KEEPERAXIONA PRODUCT
IN DEVELOPMENTiPhone + iPad · Apple App Store

Bring in the document. Keeper does most of the admin work around it.

A PDF, image or photo comes in. Keeper is designed to read it, work out what it may be, summarize it, surface important facts and dates, then suggest where it belongs and whether it needs your attention. You should not have to read, name, classify and remember every file by hand before it becomes useful.

Keeper proposes and prepares. You can review uncertain information, and anything with a real-world consequence remains under your approval.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Having the file is not enough. You still need to understand what it means.

A digital archive can store a document. The admin work starts after that: understand what arrived, pick out the important facts, decide what it belongs to and remember whether something needs to happen. Keeper is designed to remove as much of that manual work as possible.

01

What is it?

Keeper can propose a document type so you do not have to infer it from a filename.

02

What matters in it?

Important facts and dates can be surfaced, with source evidence behind important values.

03

What does it belong to?

Keeper can suggest category and relationships to a person, provider, contract, asset or matter.

04

Do I need to do anything?

If a deadline or next step is detected, Keeper can flag it separately and propose an action.

WHAT KEEPER TAKES OFF YOUR HANDS

You bring the document in. Keeper does most of the processing.

The intended user journey does not begin with building folders. It begins with a document, which Keeper gradually turns into useful, reviewable administrative information.

WHAT THIS LOOKS LIKE IN PRACTICE

The same Keeper work across very different documents.

These are illustrative examples of the planned product experience, not real user data and not automatic decisions.

INSURANCE LETTER

Do not make the user dig out the claim number and deadline.

An insurer sends a letter. On its own it is still just a PDF.

KEEPER WORK
  • recognize the kind of document
  • surface the reference and possible deadline
  • suggest the related claim or vehicle
  • flag when a reply or follow-up may be needed
INVOICE / PAYMENT NOTICE

A deadline should not remain just another line in a document.

Keeper tries to turn the document into structured, reviewable information.

KEEPER WORK
  • propose issuer, amount and date recognition
  • suggest document type and category
  • show where the deadline came from
  • propose a reminder that you approve
CONTRACT / SUBSCRIPTION

Do not rely on memory to connect old and new documents.

A contract may later receive amendments, invoices or fresh notices.

KEEPER WORK
  • recognize contract characteristics and parties
  • surface relevant dates
  • suggest relationships to earlier documents
  • flag when review or expiry may be approaching
PROPERTY / RENOVATION

Turn mixed documents into a clearer administrative picture.

Quotes, invoices, photos and warranties may belong to the same work even when they arrive in different formats.

KEEPER WORK
  • recognize different document types
  • suggest a shared project or matter context
  • surface important dates and references
  • help you return later without rebuilding the context

TRUST AND CONTROL

Keeper helps. It does not decide for you.

01

Local-first by design.

The product direction puts the device at the centre of document processing rather than treating remote processing as the default.

02

Important facts need a source.

The aim is to show what part of the document supports an extracted date or other important value.

03

Uncertainty should be visible.

If something is not reliable enough, Keeper should ask for review rather than pretend certainty.

04

Your correction comes first.

A later automatic proposal must not quietly overwrite a correction you already made.
