WHY KEEPER?

You do not need more folders. You need less searching.

A folder tells you where you put a file. That is not enough months later, when what you actually need to remember is which contract, vehicle, property or project it belonged to.

Keeper keeps those relationships together. Open a matter and you can see the original document, everything connected to it, the important dates and what comes next.

The goal is not to make you organise more.

It is to stop you having to rebuild the whole story every time you need something.

HOW YOU USE IT

From one document to one clear matter.

Bring in the document. Keeper helps place it in the right matter and connect it with what is already there.

01

Bring in the document.

PDF, image or photo. No need to rename and sort everything by hand first.

02

See what is in it.

You see more than a filename: the document type and important details are brought forward.

03

Connect what belongs together.

Letters, invoices, contracts, images and other files can belong to the same matter.

04

See what comes next.

What needs attention, what you are waiting for, what expires soon and which next step should not be forgotten.

PLANNED FEATURE · FOLDER ORGANISATION

Keeper can suggest a folder or matter for related documents and, after approval, organise them in one place.

WHERE IT HELPS

The same order, across very different matters.

The document type is not the important part. What matters is that several files, dates and actions belong to the same thing.

01 / CONTRACTS AND SUBSCRIPTIONS

See the deadline before you need to act.

Keep the contract, amendments, invoices and related correspondence in one matter.

  • original contract and amendments
  • expiry or review date
  • related correspondence
02 / VEHICLE AND INSURANCE

One claim, with its whole history together.

The policy, claim form, assessment, repair invoice and insurer correspondence belong to the same matter.

  • related documents in one matter
  • events in chronological order
  • next deadline or expected reply
03 / PROPERTY AND RENOVATION

Stop rebuilding the story from separate folders.

Quotes, contract, invoices, photos and warranty documents can stay with the work they belong to.

  • quotes and contract
  • invoices and attachments
  • handover and warranty documents
04 / SMALL BUSINESS

Keep one project's documents together.

Documents for the client, the work and the accounting side can be followed from one place.

  • quote and order
  • project documents and correspondence
  • invoices and accounting packet

FOLDERS AND RELATIONSHIPS

A folder shows where it is. A relationship shows what it belongs to.

An invoice can relate to a contract, a project or an asset at the same time. You do not need duplicate copies of the same file.

Related documents, dates and actions stay visible in the matter. Months later, you can pick up from the context you left behind instead of reconstructing it.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL

Important documents should remain yours.

01

Local-first by default.

Document handling is not built around hidden cloud uploads.

02

The original stays available.

You can always check what the document actually says.

03

Your documents are not training material.

We do not use the contents of your documents to train AI models.

04

Important decisions stay with you.

Keeper can suggest and prepare, but consequential actions require your approval.
