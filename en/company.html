Who is behind AXIONA?

AXIONA Systems is led by Asztalos Zoltán. The focus is custom internal software for small companies where daily work depends too much on email, spreadsheets and manual administration.

The company structure and service model are still under preparation. The site therefore does not promise a finished platform. It shows focus, working method and the first reference.

Operating principles

Concrete problem first

The work starts from where a company loses time, money or control, not from a technology trend.

Maintainable system

Good internal software remains understandable, fixable and does not add unnecessary burden.

Honest status

Directions in development should be communicated as directions in development, not as finished products.

First reference

The first public reference is the AXIONA website and release workflow. It is internal work, but it shows the versioned and verifiable build method.
