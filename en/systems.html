01 / SYSTEM DESIGN

Start with the operation. Not the tool.

First we need to understand what moves the process forward, where it breaks and who needs which information. Only then do we decide whether the right answer is custom business software, automation or integration, document and data workflows, a web interface, or a combination of these.

SYSTEM DESIGN AND PROCESS DESIGN

Turn scattered daily work into a traceable system.

I map how a piece of work moves from intake to completion: who handles it, which data and documents are needed, where decisions are made and what triggers the next action. The system is built around that operation instead of forcing a ready-made product onto the company.

  • process and friction-point discovery
  • roles, states and decision points
  • structured data and documents
  • system connections, integrations and automatable steps
  • clear handover and room to extend
AUTOMATION WITH BOUNDARIES

Clear, repetitive steps can be automated. Decisions stay with people wherever judgement and accountability are required.
