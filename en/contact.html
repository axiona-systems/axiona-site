06 / FIRST CONVERSATION

Tell me what is not working well today. We can choose the technology later.

A short description is enough: where work gets stuck, who needs to use the result and what would count as a useful improvement. You do not need to decide in advance whether the answer is software, automation, integration, a document workflow or a web interface.

The fields below are not submitted to a separate AXIONA form database. Your browser only prepares a structured email in your own mail application.

A good starting point is when…
  • the same data has to be copied manually between several places
  • email or spreadsheets make it difficult to see the current state of a job
  • repetitive document or administrative steps keep creating errors
  • information has to be moved manually between disconnected systems
