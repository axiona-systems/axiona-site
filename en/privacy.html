PRIVACY / CURRENT OPERATION

Data handling should be as transparent as the system itself.

This notice describes the current operation of the public axiona.systems website and direct email contact. AXIONA does not treat one country as the universal privacy baseline: the mandatory rules that actually apply to the relevant processing govern each situation.

What does the current website process?

The public site is data-minimal: there are no user accounts, no ordering or payment flow, no marketing tracking, and website traffic is measured only through an aggregate visit counter.

01

Responsible party and contact

Responsibility for processing connected with the website and direct enquiries: AXIONA Systems / Asztalos Zoltán. Privacy requests: hello@axiona.systems.

02

Website visits and counter

The website is served through GitHub Pages. On a new direct or external entry, the browser sends a content-free request to the Supabase counter endpoint. AXIONA’s own database retains only the aggregate visit total; it does not create visitor records, IP lists, device profiles, country lists or page histories. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical network data in their own logs where necessary to serve and secure requests.

03

Email enquiries

If you email us, we process your email address, any name you provide, the message content and files you voluntarily attach. We use this information to answer the enquiry, discuss projects, provide support and, where necessary, handle related legal or security matters.

04

Retention

The aggregate visit total is retained for statistical purposes, but AXIONA does not create a visitor-level history. Email data is kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry, document related work or meet legal obligations and establish or defend claims. Provider-side technical logs are retained according to the relevant provider’s current settings and terms.

05

Service providers and international processing

GitHub Pages participates in serving the website, Supabase operates the aggregate visit counter, and the email provider participates in message delivery. These providers may operate in multiple countries or regions. Where personal data is transferred internationally, the contractual or other safeguards required by the applicable data-protection law govern the transfer.

06

Cookies, profiling and automated decisions

The visit counter sets no cookie, localStorage identifier or sessionStorage identifier, sends no page name or referrer, and creates no visitor profile. AXIONA does not use advertising trackers. The website does not make automated decisions about visitors with legal or similarly significant effects.

07

Your rights

Depending on the data-protection law that applies, you may request information, access, correction or deletion and, where applicable, restriction, objection, data portability or other locally provided rights. Requests can be sent to hello@axiona.systems.

08

International legal framework

axiona.systems is not limited to a single country. The mandatory data-protection rules that apply depend on the data subject, the service and the circumstances of the processing. This notice does not reduce any rights or mandatory safeguards provided by applicable law.

Applications and software

This page does not automatically make privacy promises for every future AXIONA application. An application may refer to this notice only when its actual processing matches it; different processing requires a separate or supplementary application-specific notice that reflects the law applicable to the relevant users and markets.
