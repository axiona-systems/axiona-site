Privacy

Privacy Policy for apps and web systems.

This page explains how AXIONA Systems handles personal data received through the website, apps, support channels and related software.

AXIONA follows a data-minimisation approach: only data needed for contact, support, operation, security or legal obligations should be processed.

Summary

The website does not use advertising tracking, does not sell personal data, and the current contact/support fields only prepare an email draft locally. Data is sent only when you send the email or contact AXIONA in another way.

Scope

This policy applies to axiona.systems, support and contact flows on the website, and AXIONA apps or software that link to this page as their privacy policy.

Controller

Controller: AXIONA Systems / Asztalos Zoltán. Privacy and support contact: hello@axiona.systems. If a specific app or service needs separate terms, this will be stated on that product page or App Store listing.

Data we may process

The goal is to make clear when data may reach AXIONA and for what reason.

Contact

Name, email address, company or project name, message content and any information you voluntarily include in an email or support request.

Support and issue reports

App or page name, device, platform, version, short issue description, reproduction steps, screenshots or files if you voluntarily attach them.

Website operation

Hosting and security infrastructure may process technical logs such as IP address, browser, requested page, timestamp and security events.

App data

AXIONA apps are intended to keep user content, settings and local library data on the device unless you send it for support or contact purposes.

Diagnostics

If Apple or the operating system makes crash information available to the developer, it may include technical diagnostic data. AXIONA uses it for troubleshooting.

Payments and App Store

If an app is distributed through the App Store, download, purchase or subscription handling related to your Apple account is handled by Apple. AXIONA does not receive full payment-card details.

Apple apps and App Store requirements

This page can be used as the App Store Privacy Policy URL for AXIONA apps that link to it. The App Store may show a separate privacy label describing which data types a specific app collects, whether data is linked to the user, and whether tracking is used. If a new feature, SDK, analytics tool, cloud service or account system changes data handling, this policy and the App Store privacy answers must be updated.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Responding to contact, quote or support requests: pre-contract steps or legitimate interest.
  • Security and technical logs: legitimate interest in protecting the website and services.
  • Legal obligations: accounting, legal defence or authority-related retention where required.
  • Consent: where separate consent is requested, you may withdraw it at any time.

Retention

Contact and support messages are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, follow up, troubleshoot or protect legal interests. If there is no further relationship or mandatory retention reason, data may be deleted or anonymised. Data stored on your device is generally removed by deleting the app or using an in-app deletion feature where available.

Sharing and service providers

AXIONA does not sell personal data. Website, email, hosting, security infrastructure, App Store and operating-system providers may technically participate in transmission or processing. In such cases, processing should be limited to what is necessary.

What we currently do not do

  • No advertising tracking on axiona.systems.
  • No selling of personal data.
  • No automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects on users.
  • No intentional collection of children’s data. If a future app is directed to children, a separate policy is required.

User rights and privacy requests

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a copy of personal data held about you. Email hello@axiona.systems with the subject “Privacy request” and state which app, website or previous request it relates to. Requests are handled within a reasonable time; where the GDPR applies, the typical response period is one month.

Security

AXIONA Systems aims to keep internal system information off public pages and to keep data handling minimised, purpose-bound and reviewable. Do not send unnecessary sensitive data, passwords, secret keys or full identity documents by email.
