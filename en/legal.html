Legal framework of the public site

The site presents work and enables contact. It does not replace an individual offer, contract, licence terms or application-specific legal documents.

01

Operator and contact

02

There is no online ordering, payment or contract-formation flow on this website. An email enquiry or contact request does not by itself create an engagement contract. Scope, price, timing and responsibilities become binding when the parties expressly accept them.

Items described as “in development”, a “direction”, a “next system direction” or similar are not finished products automatically available for purchase. Features, names, timing and availability may change during actual development.

Content is maintained with reasonable care, but technical and business information can change. No guarantee is made that the website will always be continuously available or error-free. This does not exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded under mandatory law.

07

Software and applications

A specific AXIONA software product or application may have separate licence, usage, support or privacy terms. Where such product-specific documents exist, they govern that product’s use.

Privacy and security
