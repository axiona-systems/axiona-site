# R113 — Keeper-calibrated AXIONA home

## Intent
R112 proved that a structural visual redesign can be rendered and deployed reliably, but its visual tone is too aggressive for the AXIONA brand. R113 keeps the R112 information architecture and visual storytelling while recalibrating its surface language to the existing Keeper/R92 direction.

## Brand calibration
- warm paper and off-white remain the dominant surfaces
- deep petrol is a contained component surface, not a default full-section background
- orange is a sparse editorial accent
- acid green is reserved for control/status emphasis
- cyan is secondary and technical, not decorative
- solid offset shadows replace glow/floating effects
- one deliberate dark trust/operations chapter is retained
- typography keeps editorial scale but avoids R112's most extreme sizing

## Structural invariants retained from R112
- one hero + system model
- three system-story steps
- six problem taxonomy rows
- six lifecycle stages
- one trust chapter
- one Keeper case study
- one closing CTA
- zero legacy overview components

## Visual contract
The overview Playwright contract now verifies both structure and the R113 brand character at 1440px and 390px in HU/EN/DE: warm contained hero visual, petrol model card, warm story field with one dark active step, one dark trust chapter, Keeper-like off-white case-study device surface, no horizontal overflow.
